# Layout

Where each thing lives in `src/`, folder by folder. Each file's header comment says more. When you add, move or remove a file, update its entry here in the same change. Which folder may import which is in `AGENTS.md` (Boundaries).

## `src/sim/`: the whole game, deterministic and headless

Intents in (`Game/Intents`), moments out (`Game/Moments`, typed in `moments.ts`), and the one view inspector, `ReadGame` (`view.ts`). Host code imports it through `src/sim/index.ts`.

- **Core.** `schema.ts`; `tuning.ts`: the player's and enemies' numbers, `enemyKinds` and the bosses (`bossIds`), and the weapons' frame data (`MOVE`). `time.ts`: the fixed step, the run clock (`Time`), the gameplay clock (`PlayTime`) and durations; `tempo.ts`: the systems that advance both clocks, and hit-stop and slow motion. `geometry.ts`: angles, directions, nearest points and arcs on the ground. `controls.ts`: the input actions and their keyboard and mouse bindings. `index.ts` also holds one step's schedule (`update`) and `makeHeadlessRuntime`, which the tests and `lint.ts` run.
- **Enemies.** `foes.ts`: what each kind decides, and what the enemy and boss brains share (telegraph moments, shot drafts, `through`, `armSweepTime`); `enemies.ts`: `EnemyBrain`, the one state machine that plays it; `reach.ts`: where shaped attacks reach the player (a sweeping arm, a gapped ring wave, a line, pillar cover); `seams.ts`: seams that burn on the floor; `spawning.ts`: the player's and enemies' spawn drafts and the spawn point a wave's enemy comes in at; `split.ts`: a killed splitter breaking into splitlings.
- **Paths.** `paths.ts`: how enemies and bosses walk round walls, props and edges. A navcat mesh of the current terrain for each body radius, built from its collision walls and cached. `PreparePaths` builds them ahead, one a step: a room's whole fight under a door's hold (its full ink), and in play any a walking body still lacks. The host calls `warmPaths` while loading so the first fight's builds run compiled.
- **Zones.** `zones.ts`: the zones in run order (number, name, kanji, roster, boss, room count). A zone's id is also its stage's and its palette's key.
- **Player and weapons.** `weapons.ts`: ids, moves, each weapon's chain, `loadoutFor`, and the `WeaponView` the host reads. `player.ts`: the player's verbs, every weapon's moves among them as the `Attack`, `Slam` and `Charge` states. `arsenal/`: where the slam and sigils land (`aim.ts`), the live hit shapes (`shapes.ts`), darts and their reload (`darts.ts`), the weapon's and darts' views (`view.ts`). `combat.ts` resolves every hit the player lands; `projectiles.ts` flies orbs (reflected ones too) and darts; `special.ts`: KUZUSHI, the special's freeze, strikes and slow motion.
- **Rooms.** `rooms.ts`: room kinds and sides, and the map, room, door and transition views.
- **Bosses.** `bosses.ts`: names, phases, patterns and the `BossView`. `bosses/`: `brain.ts`, the `BossBrain` system (name card, patterns, shifts, balance, break, Sunder's split, defeat); `rules.ts`, the rules combat and the view share, and what each boss's shifts summon; `moons.ts`, the Orrery's moons; `spawn.ts`, spawn drafts (bosses, Sunder's half, elites); `view.ts`, the boss view; `debug.ts`, the boss debug hooks.
- **Floors.** `stages.ts` lists the stages; each zone's classic arena and its hazard is a file in `stages/` (`courtyard.ts`, `spiral.ts`, `void.ts`). The sandbox plays on these arenas and the title stands in The Courtyard's. `arena.ts`: the terrain collisions build from a layout, doorways included (an open door is a gap with its recess, a locked one a seal, a Void door a landing). `ground.ts`: the floor the player is on, and `OPEN_FLOOR`, the debug scenes' open floor; `layoutOf`, `hazardOf` and `groundOf` read `CurrentStage`, whose `room` names the current room (null on a classic arena).
- **Dungeon.** `dungeon/`:
  - `generate.ts`: the zone's rooms made from a seed by Isaac's generator, and `dungeonSeedOf`, the dungeon a run's seed makes.
  - `rooms/`: the 21 room templates, seven per zone (`courtyard.ts`, `spiral.ts`, `void.ts`); `plan.ts`: their plan type; `templates.ts`: their registry.
  - `pools.ts`: each zone's wave pools.
  - `layout.ts`: a placed room's layout, terrain, arrival point and doors.
  - `enter.ts`: what a room sets as the player arrives.
  - `transitions.ts`: the door and zone transitions.
  - `shrines.ts`: altars, springs, and picking up a clear's drops.
  - `boss-room.ts`: what a boss changes in its room (a broken pillar, the Orrery's beam).
  - `rules.ts`: the dungeon's and the run's numbers, the seed range (`SEED_MAX`) and a transition's beat order (`BEATS`) among them; `schema.ts`: its resources, `Dungeon` and `Transition`; `view.ts`: the map, room, transition and run views; `debug.ts`: the rooms' debug hooks.
- **Systems.** `flow.ts`: the run's start, pause and restart. `waves.ts`: the current room's fight (locking, waves, the clear and the drops it leaves, the boss room, the run's end). `hazards.ts`: the floor's hazard (Sunder's slabs fall by its phase); `beam.ts`: where the Spiral's beam points and reaches through a sweep. `edges.ts`: ring-outs. `debug.ts`: `DebugCheats` takes invulnerability and movement overrides on any screen, `DebugIntents` the rest during a run.

## `src/render/`

`index.ts`: `RenderSync` makes one `ActorVisual` per actor entity (from `src/look`), places it between steps with `RenderSync.interpolate`, and forwards moments to the host.

## `src/host/`

The browser host: runtime and input, the fixed-step loop on pixi's ticker and intents (`host.ts`), the moment bus (`moments.ts`), mouse aim (`aim.ts`), the post-pass stack (`post.ts`), the `HostContext` every install gets (`types.ts`), and the debug API on `window.__game` (`debug.ts`). For the lab it also scales time (`timeScale`, slow motion for everything at once) and can hold Esc, P and Enter back from the game's pause and confirm (`flowKeys`).

Loading: `host.load` warms the enemies' paths, then runs every install's `Installed.warm` steps through `src/gpu/warmup.ts` (starting over on a device change), then the handover, which waits for the GPU to drain (`drained`) and lets the title start and starts the frame clock over; it resolves `HostContext.loaded`, and keeps the compiles after it (`compiles()`), logging each as a warning or an error when asked to. Until the handover `HostContext.loading` is true: the game's keys and the camera are held. `loading.ts` drives the DOM loader `index.html` paints: its bar, its outro, its failure, `?loading=hold` and `window.__loading`.

## `src/gpu/`: compile watching and warm-up

Generic pixi code meant for upstreaming, for `main.ts` and the host: it imports only pixi.js.

- `compiles.ts`: `watchCompiles` counts every pipeline, shader module, GL shader and GL program the renderer compiles, each with a label naming it, by wrapping methods on the renderer's own GPUDevice or WebGL context. It follows a device change or a context restore, and counts those (`contextChanges`). `diffCounts` subtracts two counts.
- `warmup.ts`: `warmUp` runs loading's steps through the app's own frames: each step's texture uploads within a byte budget per frame, then its poses, each drawn until a frame compiles nothing, or listed as unsettled after `maxFramesPerPose`. Its progress never goes back, and it starts over when the context changes. `afterRender` gives it its frames: each resolves after the ticker's next render. `drained` waits until the GPU has done all the work sent to it, which on WebGPU includes the pipelines it compiles after their calls return.

## `src/camera/`

- `rig.ts` (through `klipp-pixi.ts`, which writes Klipp's shot onto the Pixi 3D camera): the Klipp rig. Named shots `title`, `play`, `pause`, `special` and `boss` (a boss's name card), shakes (scaled by the screen-shake setting), lens punches, and `cutPlay` (the play camera's cut under a transition's cover).
- `index.ts`: picks the shot from the screen; bounds the play frame per room from its floor's box (the classic arenas by `BOUNDS`; Void slabs tightening to those still standing); turns the frame toward an open zone gate and gives way on its side; keeps the HUD's bands clear through the rig's `reserve` (a boss's bar, with `pullBack`; the right column on a narrow screen); cuts on `roomSwapped`; frames a boss's name card on `bossIntro`, and the special against the current room's layout. The title shot plays the designed shots the title scene cuts to (`cutTitle`) and pans its subject into the right half, clear of the title's menu column (`frameTitle`, turned by `title-pan.ts` so a lens looking down keeps the subject where a level one does; `title-pan.ts` also holds `titleShotAt`, the eased glide of a title shot's lens and aim, which the rig and the title's `outOfShot` share); title to play is a cut, under the run's ink cover.

## `src/look/`: how the world and actors look

- **The world on screen.** `index.ts` (installLook) sets up the world, its sun, the actors' shared parts, bloom and the grade. `worlds.ts` rebuilds it when what is on screen changes: the Courtyard behind the title, a zone's classic arena in the sandbox, or the dungeon room the player is in.
- **Rooms.** `rooms/`:
  - `index.ts`: `buildRoom`, from its zone's builder: `yard.ts` (the Courtyard's walled yards), `dial.ts` (the Spiral's dials), `islands.ts` (the Void's slabs).
  - `doors.ts`: each zone's posts, seals that slam shut and break on twos, the locked emblem, the boss door, an open door's light and threshold chevrons, the zone gate and its glare of the next zone's light. A side with no door is wall.
  - `shrines.ts`: altars, the spring (a boss's rises in place) and a clear's drops.
  - Each template's set pieces: `yard-pieces.ts` (the Courtyard's banners, wall lamps, a fallen column), `dial-pieces.ts` (the Spiral's dial faces, armillaries, gnomon blades), `island-pieces.ts` (the Void's column stumps, rock clusters, rifts).
  - Each template's floor is an SVG (`yard-floor.ts`, `dial-floor.ts`, `islands-floor.ts`), drawn once per zone into `art.ts`'s cache, prepared when the zone starts (The Courtyard's while loading, which waits for them).
  - `kit.ts`: the door icons, altar kanji, drops and other room sprites; `build.ts`: the shared build context, `ZoneLook` (what a zone's builder returns), the gate's side, mirrored floor drawings, and `whenDrawn`, which hands a room its floor drawing when it lands after the room was built; `seed.ts`: a steady seed from a name, so a room draws the same every time it is built.
- **Classic arenas.** The Courtyard in `arena.ts` and `floor.ts`; The Spiral in `spiral.ts` and `spiral-floor.ts` (its columns, spire, ring wall and fade shared with the dial rooms in `dial-parts.ts`); The Void in `void.ts` and `void-floor.ts` (its slabs, far scenery, drifting debris, column of light, sun and fades shared with the island rooms in `void-parts.ts`). All built from `parts.ts` (which also holds `SUNKEN`, how far the ground outside the walls sits below the floor, and `KERB`, a south wall's kerb height), with wear from `marks.ts`.
- **Light and ink.** `lighting.ts`: each world aims the one sun. `shaders/ink.ts`: the ink material (each surface's fade into the void and a stage's red light are its own) and each zone's fill light (`ZONE_FILL`); `setInkCut` thins a surface to a dither, as the Spiral's spire and pillars do while they hide the action. `concrete.ts`: the concrete grain tile it maps over every surface; `shaders/noise.ts`: the noise behind its grain, wear and brush strokes. `shaders/sprite.ts`: unlit sprite and lamp materials. `occlusion.ts`: whether a world's solid stands between the camera and an actor (`propBetween`).
- **Actors.** `createActorVisual`. `actors.ts`: the hero's pose through each weapon's moves, the slam's hop, the charge folding it toward its dart pose, and the dart's needle; the orbiter's ring, the warden's shell, the lancer's hover. `foes.ts`: the sweeper's arm, the pulsar's spin, the leaper's arc, the cutter's hover, the elite's crown. `bosses.ts`: the bosses' compositions. `needle.ts`: Sunder's needles. Every enemy is drawn in its own zone's accent (`paletteOfKind`). `shapes.ts`: each body's outline, art size, height and rim; `geometry.ts`: the solids built from them (extruded outlines, the folded arrowhead, rings); `glyphs.ts`: the top faces' glyph art and the soft sprites under them (shadow, glow, orbs, streaks, flecks); `shaders/actor.ts`: the body material (glowing top, two-step light, rim, hit flash, ghost) and the health bar's.
- **Loading.** `warm.ts`: what loading hands the GPU for look: its textures, the room kit and The Courtyard's room floors, which it waits for.
- **Air and grade.** `atmosphere.ts`: the air's motes per zone. `post.ts`, with the grade's shader in `grade.ts`: each zone's tone ramp from `tones.ts`, the Courtyard's red repainted as the zone's accent outside it, and the Courtyard's plain ramp and red on the title. `twos.ts`: the twelve-drawings-a-second timing (`DRAWINGS`, `onTwos`, `keyed`) that the actors, set pieces, doors, shrines, stonework, air and grain step on. `textures.ts`: the look's textures, rasterised once at install (`loadTextures`).

## `src/vfx/`: transient effects

Driven by moments and view data; may shake the camera and choreograph the special through the rig.

- `index.ts` (installVfx) loads the kit and installs every effect below; loading hands its kit's and the weapons' kit's textures to the GPU. `fx.ts`: `Fx`, what every effect module draws with (the particle layers, cues, arcs, screen, frame controls and impacts), and the helpers they share (`drawnAt`, `spray`). `cues.ts`: the game and real effect clocks, and cues held on their drawings (`ON_TWOS`). `kit.ts`: every texture the effects draw with, rasterised once from `draw/` (`loadKit`, `framesOf`); vfx owns it, and the title borrows the same one.
- `particles.ts`: `SpriteLayer`, a pool of hand-driven sprites on one particle container. `arcs.ts`: crescents bent along a tapering strip, pooled in `ArcPool` and revealed by `shaders.ts` (`arcBit`; `telegraphBit` fills a telegraph).
- `draw/`: the SVG drawings. `pen.ts`: seeded strokes, tapers and ink filters; `atlas.ts`: every small drawing on one sheet; `shapes.ts`: the actors' silhouettes and their flashes, halves and fragments; `strips.ts`: the slash stroke and its glow, the erosion noise, telegraph lines and rings, and the screen's focus lines, slash and vignette.
- `impacts.ts`: rationed full-screen impact frames and the vignette pulses of small blows; `frame.ts`: the impact kinds and the focus blur that vfx sets on `HostContext.frame`.
- `combat.ts`: hits, kills, parries, reflections and the player's hurts; `shards.ts`: a kill's white flash, cut and shatter. `enemies.ts`: how enemies arrive, blink, launch, slam and shoot. `trails.ts`: the dash's afterimages and dotted trail, and each orb's glow and tail. `ambient.ts`: a fight's air, shard lines on the wind and rising embers. `special.ts`: KUZUSHI's freeze, strikes, strobes and shatter.
- `beam.ts`: the Spiral's beam and its floor of danger and cover (read from the room's floor and pillars), its shader bits in `beam-shaders.ts`.
- `exits.ts`: the frame-edge chevrons pointing to a cleared room's open doors off the frame.
- `screen.ts`: the flat layer over the frame (strobes, vignette pulses, focus lines, cinema bars, in the zone's accent and ink).
- `edges.ts`: ring-outs and the Void's cracking and falling slabs.
- `foes.ts`: the Spiral's and the Void's enemies' orbits, clanks, volleys, open windows, lock-ons, charges, splits, sweeps, leaps and landings.
- `waves.ts`: ring waves. `seams.ts`: seams.
- `telegraphs.ts`: telegraph lines, rings, arcs and spots (each in its attacker's zone accent, a boss's several at once), over the reshapeable ground bands of `bands.ts`, which also holds `overlayMaterial`, the flat ground-overlay material that telegraphs, waves and seams share.
- `bosses.ts`: the bosses' name cards, shifts, breaks, stuns, splits, moon cuts and defeats.
- `doors.ts`: the doors' slams and breaks.
- `transitions.ts`: the door and zone transitions (the ink wipe, the held hero, speed lines, the outlines reveal), with their drawings in `draw/transitions.ts`.
- `slashes.ts`: the blade's crescents. `weapons/`: every other weapon's effects, installed by its `index.ts` with the flourish of a weapon coming into hand (the breaker's cleave, slam mark and landing in `breaker.ts`; the darts' shots, trails, aim line and pierce in `darts.ts`, the streak and aim line laid flat by `strip.ts`'s `GroundStrip`; the sigils' rings, glyphs, bursts and stamps in `sigils.ts`), their textures in `weapons/kit.ts` and `draw/weapons.ts`, coloured by the zone's accent.

## `src/ui/`: the UI, drawn in Pixi

Every screen is drawn in Pixi on `layers.ui` and animated with GSAP. In play it takes the zone's accent; the title keeps the Courtyard's red.

- **Install.** `index.ts` (installUi) stacks the screens in slots, bottom to top: `play` (the HUD, the altar's card, the boss's bar, banners, the map over play), `cards` (a boss's name card, the zone card), `screens` (pause, results), the launch's ink cover, the title, the cut between screens and `readout` (the FPS). `play`, `cards` and `readout` never take the pointer, so presses reach the game; `layers.ui` takes it only while a modal screen (title, pause, results) is up. It enters no screen until loading ends, so the title's menu plays its intro as the loader lifts. A screen is `Staged` (`screen.ts`): laid out at install and on every resize, and disposed with the UI; `screen.ts` also has `piece`, a slot that layout places holding a body that motion moves. A `Tinted` one is recoloured when the zone's accent changes (`accent.ts`: `accentOf`; what sits over play takes `greyOf` it under pause). Every menu's highlight is a `Cursor` (`cursor.ts`): it opens on the first option and ignores the pointer until it moves. The menus play their moves, choices and backs through `src/art/sounds.ts`.
- **Kit.** `kit/` is what the screens build from:
  - `viewport.ts`: margin, type scale, narrow. `type.ts`: the type roles and display faces, white styles coloured by `tint` (`scaleType`, `refreshFonts`), `tracked` letter-spacing tweens, CSS line metrics (`placeInLine`, `inlineWidth`), `textIn` (a tinted text in a given style), `ownedFaces` (the faces one screen owns, released together) and `INK_SHADOW` (the HUD's and boss bar's text shadow). `digits.ts`: BitmapText figures with tabular digits, for numbers that change often.
  - `ink.ts`: brush strokes. `inkContext` for opaque fills only, `inkLayer` for a translucent one, `spineReveal` to draw one on along its spine, `brushRaster` for a thin brush or its glow rasterised by the browser at its size on screen. `glow.ts`: blurred SVG glows, each key drawn once and shared. `layer.ts`: `createLayer`, a group cached as one texture, and `eraseRamp`, a fade erased inside one.
  - `wipe.ts`: masks wiping in from an edge. `scrim.ts`: radial veils. `drain.ts`: the scene drained to grey through the frame's grade, one shared by the title, pause and results.
  - `corners.ts`: ticks and brackets, with their `pop` entrance and `kick` on a choice. `keycap.ts`: keycaps, hints and `backAction` (the Esc BACK hover and click the sheet and panel share). `icon.ts`: icons from `icons.ts` markup as shared contexts.
  - `menu.ts`: the menu (an accent brush bar fading right inside corner brackets). `sheet.ts`: the title's framed sheet with swapped bodies. `panel.ts`: a framed panel with a head. `plate.ts`: the panels' ink plate, blurring the scene behind it.
  - `sink.ts`: the hidden modal element that holds a screen's keyboard, and `blockPointer`, which keeps presses on a modal screen from the game.
- **Brushes and fades.** `brush.ts`: `brushStroke`, the seeded hand-made outlines every brush is drawn from, `svgPaths`, those outlines as SVG paths, and `lean`, brush lettering's forward slant. A brush or overlapping fill that is translucent or a gradient goes in a Layer: Pixi fills every subpath of one path on its own, so overlaps double and a gradient restarts at every bristle. To fade overlapping opaque pieces as one (the slash's red, shards and core), tween an AlphaFilter's alpha on their parent, never the parent's own. A mask on a cached node renders nothing (mask a parent), and a Layer refreshed under a filtered ancestor caches nothing (tint instead).
- **Title.** `title.ts`, laid out by the pure `title-layout.ts`, is drawn straight over the scene: the wordmark (`wordmark.ts`: SVG letters from the strokes in `letters.ts`, swell, slash and core rasterised at the size shown, revealed by a mask of the strokes' spines while they write) and 崩し top left, the large menu centred down the left half, and the keys along the foot. Each menu item opens its body on a sheet over the right half (the whole screen when narrow), and drains and dims the scene. The bodies are `panels/`: the zones (`zones.ts`: a card per zone in its own palette with its plan, drawn by `plan.ts`, later zones open once reached), controls and settings (`controls.ts`, `settings.ts`; pause shows both, compact) and extras (`extras.ts`: the lab's `registerExtra` entries). `records.ts`: what localStorage keeps (zones reached, each zone's best clear, the best full run). `settings-rows.ts` and `moves.ts`: the settings' rows and the controls' moves and bindings. A chosen zone's run starts under the ink cover of `launch.ts`, split along the screen-wide slash; `COVER_LIFT_SECONDS` is when play shows whole, which the HUD and the first banner wait for.
- **Play.** `hud.ts`: diamonds, FOCUS, the combo, the zone, timer and the room's kind, seal and wave; the weapon in hand (`weapon-hud.ts`) and the minimap (`minimap.ts`). `map.ts` draws the dungeon's rooms, doors, seals and gate as Pixi cells for the minimap and `full-map.ts` (the full map with its legend); `room-icons.ts`: the room kinds' icons; `map-overlay.ts`: the full map over play on Tab or M, docked away from the hero. `altar.ts`: the altar's prompt, held while a banner plays. `banner.ts`: the run's first zone, each wave, a wave's and a room's clear with its drops, a zone's clear. `boss-bar.ts`: a boss's bar, fading back while the boss stands under it; `boss-card.ts`: its name card. `zone-card.ts`: the zone change's ink sheet and card. `fps.ts`: the FPS readout. `format.ts`: the timer's `MM:SS.cc` and two-digit numbers.
- **Screens over play.** `pause.ts`: pause, with the map, controls and settings opening beside it. `results.ts`: run clear and game over, its seal rasterised in both forms in every zone's accent at install. `sweep.ts`: the slash between screens.
- **Loading.** installUi hands the results' seals to the GPU while loading.
- **Clocks.** The boss's name card and the zone card are scrubbed by the view's progress, so they hold and step with `__game.freeze` and `step`; everything else runs on GSAP's clock, which `freeze` pauses. `motion.ts`: eases held on twos like hand-drawn animation (`drawn`), `rattle` for a hit, and `shown`, which fades a node in or out.
- **What stays DOM.** The keyboard sinks, under the overlay's `.k-ui` root, and what the lab borrows: the DOM menu (`menu.ts`, whose `FILL` and `SLASH` strokes the kit's menu draws too), the DOM panels' `ticks` (`panel.ts`), `el` and its helpers (`dom.ts`), `makeModal` (`screen.ts`), `extras.ts` (`registerExtra`, which adds an entry to EXTRAS, and `hidePlayUi`, the lab's HUD toggle) and `ui.css`, which installUi imports for the lab.

## `src/title/`

The title's 3D scene, shown only while the flow is Title: the hero in a closing ring of red shapes under a huge red sun, drawn by look's actor visuals from views it writes itself; a sakuga beat on a loop cut between designed shots (`staging.ts`, `director.ts`; the subject holds the middle of the right half, beside the title's menu and under an open sheet, and centres on a narrow screen); and the hero's dash into play when a zone is chosen. `index.ts` (installTitle) shows it on the title and hides it off it; while loading it holds at t = 0, and it starts its loop as loading ends. Loading hands its textures to the GPU. `cast.ts`: the hero, the ring of shapes and their telegraph lines; `set.ts`: the sun and the cloud shadows, drawn in `textures.ts`; `effects.ts`: vfx's drawings (and vfx's kit, borrowed) in layers of the title's own, on its clock. `window.__title.time()` is its loop clock, for screenshot steps; it lands once loading ends.

## `src/audio/`: music and sound effects

- `index.ts` (installAudio): plays a sound on each of a few moments and the music for what is on screen, through `src/art/sounds.ts` and @pixi/sound; its warm step waits for the files to load; the first key starts audio.
- `sfx/<name>.wav` and `music/<track>.mp3`: the sound effects and the music loops. Each MP3 holds its loop and then the loop's first second again, so the game loops it from 0.5 s to its end less 0.5 s, clear of the encoder's delay.

## `src/art/`: shared art helpers

- `svg.ts`: `svgTexture`, an SVG string to a pixi Texture, and `svgTextures` for several at once.
- `palette.ts`: the Courtyard's `PALETTE` (the title and menus use it) and each zone's `ZONE_PALETTES`, looked up with `paletteOf(zone)`, or `paletteOfKind` for an enemy's zone; `hex`, `css` and `rgbOf` (sRGB channels) write a colour out. `color.ts`: `linearOf`, a colour in linear RGB for material values, apart from palette.ts so pure modules can read colours without loading @pixi/3d.
- `arrowhead.ts`: the player's arrowhead outline and its notch (`NOTCH`, `DASH_NOTCH` through a dash), which every drawing of it takes.
- `frame.ts`: the whole-frame look controls (impact frames, flashes, focus blur, the outlines a room inks in with). The host makes one set and passes it to every install as `HostContext.frame`; vfx sets it and look's grade pass draws it.
- `ground.ts`: the floor on screen (`HostContext.ground`). Look's world flags a pale floor, and orbs and telegraph lines draw with ink edges on it.
- `depth.ts`: the draw-order bands (the player over everything, effects over the world, an enemy behind a prop drawn through it).
- `settings.ts`: the player's settings (screen shake, impact flashes, film grain, FPS readout, the music's and sound effects' volumes), kept in localStorage; the camera, vfx, look and audio read them.
- `title-cues.ts`: the channel from the title menu to the title scene.
- `sounds.ts`: the sound effects (`SFX`) and tracks (`TRACKS`) with their files, `loadSounds` (registers them with @pixi/sound, loads them and keeps their volumes on the settings) and `play`, which the UI and audio use.

## `src/lab/`: the debug scenes

Opened with `?lab`, `?lab=<scene>`, or EXTRAS on the title. Each scene runs in a zone, set up through the debug API on a sandbox run (grid or the zone's arena) or on a dungeon run it starts itself (`Scene.start`). `window.__lab` opens scenes and works their controls for screenshot scripts.

- `index.ts` (installLab): the lab and `window.__lab`. `scene.ts`: the `Scene` contract and the `LabTools` a running scene gets (moments through `on`, dropped when the lab stops the run); `scenes.ts`: the scenes in list order.
- `scenes/`: one file per scene (`special.ts`; `attacks.ts`: player attacks with any weapon; `gallery.ts`: enemy gallery by zone with its boss; `threats.ts`: enemy attacks and boss patterns; `bosses.ts`: bosses in their rooms; `movement.ts`; `zones.ts`: zones and their palettes; `rooms.ts`: rooms and hazards; `dungeon.ts`; `doors.ts`: doors and transitions); `roster.ts`: what they share: the kinds, the seals toggle, a doorway's trigger line and arrival arrow, and small helpers (`settler`, `ringAround`, `facingTo`).
- `panel.ts`: the docked panel; `list.ts`: the scene list; `floor.ts`: the measuring-grid floor; `lab.css`: the lab's DOM styles.
- `camera.ts`: the lab cameras added to the rig's Klipp and their presets (`cameraPresets`), and the play camera's subject kept beside the panel (through the rig's `inset`).
- `sketch.ts`, `hitboxes.ts`: hit shapes and scene marks drawn on the ground through the live camera; `hitboxes.ts` also keeps each hit shape the weapon puts live.
- `timeline.ts`: the frame timeline; `frames.ts`: steps and seconds, each move's name, and the player's state and attack frame as labels; `keys.ts`: the keys; `store.ts`: what it remembers; `choose.ts`: `oneOf`, `isOneOf` and `after`, which narrow a stored or typed value to a fixed list and step round one.
- `dungeons.ts`: any seed's dungeon planned ahead (the run seed's dungeon, the way to the boss, the seed that places a template, a start room with four doors); `map.ts`: the map it draws.

## Elsewhere

- `index.html`: the page, with the loader's markup and styles inline (a symbol for its brush stroke, the ink in two halves each holding a copy of the meter, the slash), so it paints before any script.
- `src/main.ts`: under the loader, wires the app, the compile watch (made before anything draws), the view, the layers (the UI's 2D `ui` over vfx's `screen` over the 3D view), the rig, the host and the installs (look, vfx, camera, ui, title, audio, then the lab with the debug API), and hands the UI and the title scene one `TitleCues` channel; then starts the host, loads (`host.load`), marks `kuzushi:loaded` and plays the loader's outro. `?compiles` makes a compile after loading a console warning, `?compiles=strict` a console error. The renderer is WebGPU where the browser has it, else WebGL (`?renderer=webgl` forces WebGL). The canvas renders at `min(DPR, 2)` (`RESOLUTION_CAP`) and follows the window to a screen of another density; above DPR 1 the canvas has no MSAA (the UI's shapes are SmoothGraphics), and the 3D view draws at resolution 1 and keeps its MSAA through `renderer.view.antialias`, which @pixi/3d reads when it builds its graph. On WebGL the renderer draws through a back buffer (`useBackBuffer`), which the UI's plates need to blur what is under them.
- `src/lint.ts`: the `ritmo-devtools lint` entry.
- `tests/`: every test. Most run the game headless, script input and intents and assert what a player would see; the rest check pure modules (the title's layout and staging, the menu cursor, records, brush strokes, formats, tones; `audio.test.ts` checks that the sound and track lists and the audio files name the same sounds and music) and, with fake devices and frames, the compile watch and the warm-up. `harness.ts` has the helpers.
